# Fin candidates

Open `attached-fin.woby`. The closed tetrahedron has one triangular fin attached
at a non-manifold edge. Expect one Woby fin candidate: source triangle 5, area 6,
two physical boundary edges, one cut boundary edge, and area ratio 1.
The closed shell has no physical boundary and is excluded from the denominator.
A maximum area ratio below 1 excludes this candidate.

These are heuristic candidates, not a repair recommendation or GGM/GMM parity.
