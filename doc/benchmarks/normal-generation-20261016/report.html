Each cell gives the median and range from three fresh processes. The builds ran in alternating order with warm input. The replay loads and copies the mesh before its timer, then runs production normal generation. Full import and viewer measurements are separate runs. Their times must not be added.

The baseline includes current main (8b5a7d9) and the completed annotation changes (5b0ad07). Both builds use Legion Balance Mode. The 14 October audit used Performance Mode; its absolute times are not the baseline for this comparison.

Implementation

  1. Clear and normalise independent vertex blocks in parallel under the existing shared worker limit.
  2. Calculate four face normals per SIMD operation on the x64 build. Keep exact square roots, division, expression order, equal face weights and degenerate-face fallbacks. Other CPU targets retain the scalar calculation.
  3. Process 16,384-face batches with at most three helpers and the caller. Calculate upcoming batches while the current batch adds its normals. An ordered hand-off preserves every face and corner addition.
  4. Use at most 768 KiB of temporary normal storage. Small meshes use the simple serial loop.
  5. Keep progress callbacks on the caller. On failure, wake waiting batches and join all workers before returning.

Correctness and resource use

  • The Release build has no compiler warnings. All 105 CTest entries pass: 95 default, 9 slow and 1 graphics. The focused normal checks pass 32 cases and 7,999 assertions.
  • Normal digests match in every replay on all three models. The full geometry and colour fingerprints match in every import. Tests compare normal bits against the original serial algorithm, including shared vertices, batch tails, signed zero, nonfinite inputs, degenerate faces, progress and cancellation.
  • All three baseline screenshots and two candidate screenshots are byte-identical. The remaining candidate differs in four pixels of the bottom-left grid-spacing label, outside the model and grid. Open the verified render.
  • Normal-pass CPU time rises from 2.094 to 2.609 CPU seconds (24.6%). This reduces latency at the cost of more CPU time. Median viewer peak private commit is 9.523 → 9.524 GiB.
